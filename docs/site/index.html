Community-run social media

Not a company cloud. A handful of inexpensive relays that only move ciphertext.

Control

The community holds the pipes

Whoever operates a few VPS nodes decides how people reach each other. Accounts, posts, and keys stay on user devices. There is no central social database to seize or sell.

Cost

A small fleet of cheap VPS

Relays are connectivity only. A modest box (or a few regional ones) can carry encrypted circuits for thousands of concurrent sessions when bulk media goes direct after hole punching.

Trust

Cryptographic messages

Operators see packets, not content. MSG is ciphertext; ACK is a delivery receipt. The relay cannot read, rank, or store a social graph.

Module 03

DCUtR hole punching

After meeting on a relay, peers punch a direct path. Video and files move as 512 KiB chunks on that mapping. NAT does not cap bytes; UDP mappings usually die after tens of seconds idle. If the hole closes, DCUtR punches again until every chunk is on disk.

BlobChunkRequest · 512 KiB · punch again if mapping dies

NAT keeps homes private

Two devices behind consumer routers usually cannot dial each other by IP alone. Without help, the apps cannot open a path.

From first open to direct media

The welcome screen asks how you are joining. There is no global people search and no public relay list on this site.

Once a relay session and reservation exist, DCUtR opens a direct path. Chunks ride that mapping. If NAT idle-timeouts the hole, Inertia punches again until the file is complete.

  1. 1Create identity on your device (keys + display name).
  2. 2Pick a path: I have an invite (the signed link already carries a relay multiaddr) or I'm starting a network (you paste a relay multiaddr; Inertia dials it).
  3. 3Open a relay session and hold a reservation.
  4. 4Sync messages over the circuit; pull media as 512 KiB chunks on a hole-punched path, and punch again if the NAT mapping expires.
